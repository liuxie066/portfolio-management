## Architecture, Best-practice, Optimality, Overengineering, and Coupling Review

- Architecture boundary: registry metadata, domain calculations, and runtime datasets remain separate; storage-backed builders stay in application/repository layers.
- Best practice: missing/null/value, partial/unknown outcomes, fresh readback, CAS/journal recovery, and fail-closed historical evidence are testable contracts.
- Optimal solution: the plan extends existing repository, summary, NAV, snapshot, compensation, and journal components; it does not introduce a schema DSL, formula engine, distributed coordinator, or new storage service.
- Overengineering: immutable values are limited to demonstrated TOCTOU/authority/replay failures; broader concurrency and historical reconstruction are deferred with explicit evidence requirements.
- Overcoupling: the 12 slices isolate static structure, each financial domain, runtime handoff, and recovery. Later slice dependencies are explicit, and no intermediate slice is independently released.
