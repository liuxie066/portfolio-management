## Goal, non-goals, and success signal review

The plan has one coherent goal: stop comparing Futu's original-currency cash
observations with a PM record whose value is already aggregated in CNY. It does
not weaken the unrelated stock, cost-basis, MMF, price, FX, account-mapping, or
NAV-finality gates. It explicitly retains raw source evidence and the separately
confirmed cash-flow-ledger workflow.

The success signals are independently testable at the readback, durable receipt,
quality artifact, NAV policy, public API, and rendered receipt boundaries. The
plan does not require or authorize a production migration.

## Assumptions tested

1. **There are only two automatic Futu cash-effect bridge call sites.**
   Confirmed by source search in `src/service/application.py` and
   `src/app/account_nav_recorder_service.py`; the helper definition itself may
   remain for historical/explicit workflows without an automatic caller.
2. **The old per-currency assumption is cross-layer, not reconciler-only.**
   Confirmed in `FutuSyncReconciler`, `FutuBalanceSyncService`, quality policy,
   quality artifact construction, HTTP freshness dependencies, receipt rendering,
   tests, and design/operator docs.
3. **Futu observation and PM holdings ownership can remain separate.**
   `FutuBalanceSyncService` already labels the cash stage `observe_only`, while
   stock/ETF and MMF remain the writable/reconciled provider replicas.
4. **The aggregate quality replacement can fail closed without pretending to
   prove the amount.** The planned contract checks only the local identity/type/
   currency/finite-value shape and uses a new dataset ID and reason code rather
   than relabeling an old broker-equality verdict.
5. **Cash-flow ledger effects must not be deleted.** `NavRecordService` uses that
   subsystem for generated-field and unresolved-effect gates independently of
   the Futu observation bridge; the plan preserves it.
6. **Old receipts cannot safely alias into the new contract.** Their
   `pm.securities_cash=trusted` claim means per-currency equality, not aggregate
   record structure. Failing closed until a new sync receipt is produced is the
   safer version-skew behavior.

## Architecture boundary review

The plan keeps provider observation in the Futu adapter/evidence boundary,
provider replica checks in the reconciler, consumer policy in the quality layer,
and user-visible explanation in the receipt renderer. The aggregate structural
fact is attached to the same readback receipt so the existing NAV write boundary
can consume durable evidence without adding a second mutable quality authority.
Its distinct dataset ID and reason code prevent it from being mistaken for a
broker-value comparison.

No new storage schema, remote writer, FX service, or bidirectional dependency is
introduced.

## Best-practice review

- The change is fail-closed for official NAV and public freshness.
- Retry is restricted to conditions that can plausibly converge after readback;
  static aggregate-record shape does not trigger a pointless wait.
- Legacy receipt version skew fails closed instead of silently coercing semantics.
- Negative tests prove forbidden reads and effects calls, not only the happy
  output shape.
- Production data and deployment are explicitly outside the implementation
  authority.

## Optimal-solution review

The rejected alternatives are materially weaker:

- Treating `pm.securities_cash` as always trusted would hide the false comparison
  but create fabricated evidence.
- Removing CASH from NAV quality entirely would weaken a required NAV input.
- Splitting Futu values into new holdings would contradict the selected PM model
  and require an undefined FX-time/migration contract.
- Deleting the entire cash-flow effect subsystem would conflate broker
  observation drift with deliberate ledger-driven aggregate CASH mutations.

The new dataset identity is the smallest safe way to make the changed semantics
explicit across existing consumers.

## Overengineering review

The plan adds no new service, schema, migration, workflow state, feature flag,
or generic abstraction. One verdict helper, dataset-identity migration, two
call-site removals, receipt wording, and contract tests are proportional to the
cross-layer defect.

## Overcoupling review

The plan avoids coupling raw currency observations to aggregate PM quantity.
The remaining coupling between a current Futu receipt and aggregate structural
evidence is deliberate: the receipt is the existing durable readback authority
consumed by the NAV gate. A separate local artifact would add a second freshness
and persistence lifecycle without improving the selected behavior.
