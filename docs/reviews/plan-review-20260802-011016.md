## Special lens conclusions

- **Architecture boundary**: role resolution remains configuration-owned; table
  and event transports share one justified authority, while conversation delivery
  remains separate.
- **Best practice**: credential-first resolution, no secret comparison, redacted
  shadow detection, pre-write feature probing, and explicit migration states are
  implementable and testable.
- **Optimal solution**: systemd credentials fit the current self-hosted Linux
  target without prematurely introducing a hosted secret service.
- **Overengineering**: no third app, multi-tenant store, OAuth UI, or generic
  provider registry is added.
- **Overcoupling**: per-unit grants follow current call paths; future receipt-flow
  separation can reduce grants without changing the two-role contract.
